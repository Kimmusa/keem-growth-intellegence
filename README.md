Keem Growth Intelligence (KG Intelligence) is an intelligent business growth companion designed to help business leaders discover opportunities, improve business performance, manage daily responsibilities and make informed decisions.
The platform combines artificial intelligence, market research, location intelligence, industry analysis, task management and personalised business recommendations.
Unlike conventional task management applications, GrowthPilot AI proactively researches the user's business environment and recommends relevant activities that can improve sales, customer acquisition, operational efficiency and business expansion.
Product mission
To make intelligent business growth guidance accessible to every business leader through personalised, actionable and continuously updated AI intelligence.
Core product promise
Every day, the platform should help answer three questions:
What should I be doing today?
What opportunities am I missing?
What can I do to improve my business?
